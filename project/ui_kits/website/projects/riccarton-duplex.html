The brief

Two units, both for long-term rental, designed to outlast their first tenancy.

A long-time investor client, downsizing their wider portfolio but wanting one more new build in a tightly-held suburb. The brief was specific: two units, three bedrooms plus study, premium finishes, fifteen-plus year hold horizon.

The work

What BF Property is doing.

Currently at month seven of a thirteen-month programme. Both units are closed-in. Fit-out begins next month with practical completion targeted for September 2026.

Programme includes a body corp setup at settlement even though the owner intends to retain both units indefinitely, on the basis that future sale flexibility matters more than the cost saving.
