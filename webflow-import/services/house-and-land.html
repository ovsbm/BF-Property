What it is

A different way into the BF Property model: we do the front end, you commit to the finished result.

Most of our work starts with a client and their land. House and land is the reverse: we identify a site, develop the build to consent-ready, and offer the bundled package to a client who wants the finished outcome without the design phase.

These are not first-home-buyer packages from a national chain. They are deliberately small. Usually two- or three-unit configurations on a single site. Offered to one client at a time.

Availability is irregular. Two to four packages per year, typically.

What is included

How a house-and-land package works.

  1. Step 01

    BF identifies site

    Site sourcing, due diligence, conditional offer or option taken.

  2. Step 02

    Design to consent-ready

    Architect, engineer, council pre-app, consent lodgement.

  3. Step 03

    Package offered

    Indicative scope and price published to BF contact list.

  4. Step 04

    Client signs

    Single client commits. Site purchased, contract signed, deposit paid.

  5. Step 05

    Build

    BF Property runs the full build under the standard fixed-price head contract.

  6. Step 06

    Handover

    Completed units handed over, titles in client name.

When this fits

Best fits for house and land.

This fits if

  • You want a BF Property build but you do not want to source land.
  • You want a fixed price before you commit.
  • You are flexible on suburb and site characteristics.
  • You are prepared to act quickly when a package is offered.

This is not right if

  • You want a one-of-one custom home. See Custom design.
  • You want the cheapest house-and-land you can find. Talk to a volume builder.
  • You already own land. See Design and build instead.
