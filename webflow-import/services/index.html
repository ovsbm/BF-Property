Not sure which

A short decision guide.

Have a site and want to build on it? Design and build is the default. If the site can carry more than one home and you want multiple units, look at Multi-unit or Medium-density depending on scale.

Have a site with an old house on it? Knock-down-and-rebuild. We assess whether the numbers favour replace over renovate.

Have a site but want to split it? Subdividing. We only do this as part of a build, not on its own.

Want a one-of-one home? Custom design, with your chosen architect.

Have land but not sure what to do with it? Sell us your land. We assess it for free and you keep the result.

Want a finished package without sourcing land? House and land. Periodically available.

Still not sure? That is normal.

A 45-minute conversation usually clarifies which service you actually need. The conversation costs nothing.
