What it is

Right-sized for a single residential site, right-sized for one senior team.

Most BF Property projects are two- to four-unit terraces on residential sites between 400 m² and 800 m². It is the configuration that fits Christchurch Medium Residential and Residential Suburban Density zoning, and the configuration capital owners ask for most.

Each unit is typically three-bedroom, single-garage, two-storey. Brick and lightweight cladding. Long-run roof. Built for long-term tenancy or staggered owner-occupier sale.

Body corporate set up and handed over at settlement if you choose titled units. Stratum-in-freehold by default.

What is included

A typical three-unit programme.

  1. Month 0

    Site assessment

    Feasibility, indicative yield, consent risk, indicative cost.

  2. Months 1–3

    Design and consent

    Architectural and engineering coordination. Resource and building consent.

  3. Months 4–7

    Build to closed-in

    Demolition if needed, foundations, framing, weathertightness.

  4. Months 8–11

    Fit-out

    Linings, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, electrical and plumbing fit-off.

  5. Months 12–13

    Landscape and CCC

    Driveways, fencing, planting, council inspections, code compliance certificate.

  6. Month 14

    Handover

    Body corp set up, keys to owner or property manager, defects period commences.
