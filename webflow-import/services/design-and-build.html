What it is

One contract, one team, one programme. Consent to handover.

Design and build is the model BF Property was built around. You bring the land or we source it for you. We coordinate the architect and engineer, run consent, hold the head contract for build, and hand over completed units.

The contract is fixed price once consent is in. The programme is yours to see weekly. The contact is one senior person from the first conversation. No tendering between us and the trades. No surprises at month nine.

This is the bread and butter. Almost every other service on this site is a variation of this one.

What is included

Six phases. We run all six.

  1. Phase 01

    Feasibility

    Site assessment, yield, council pre-app, indicative build cost. No charge for the first one.

  2. Phase 02

    Detailed design

    Architect and engineer coordination through to consent-ready drawings. Specification locked.

  3. Phase 03

    Consent

    Resource and building consent lodged, tracked, and granted. We deal with council, not you.

  4. Phase 04

    Pre-construction

    Trades booked, programme finalised, fixed-price contract issued.

  5. Phase 05

    Build

    BF Property holds the head contract. Senior site lead on the ground daily. Fortnightly reports to you.

  6. Phase 06

    Handover

    Code compliance, defects period, body corp setup if multi-unit. You receive the completed units.

When this fits

Best fits for design and build.

This fits if

  • You have capital and want an income-producing or owner-occupied finished asset.
  • You do not want to project-manage trades yourself.
  • You value certainty over the lowest possible cost.
  • You can sign at four or five decision gates and leave the rest to us.
  • Project value sits above $1m, typically $1m to $5m.

This is not right if

  • You are building under $1m, one home, owner-occupied. A volume builder fits better.
  • You want to act as your own builder and coordinate trades yourself.
  • You are looking for the cheapest possible square-metre rate.
  • The site is outside Canterbury.
