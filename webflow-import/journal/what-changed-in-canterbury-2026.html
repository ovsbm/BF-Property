What changed in Canterbury, 2026.

Twelve-month snapshot of build cost, consent timelines, and yield expectations across the suburbs we work in. Plain numbers, no spin.

Build cost

Indicative all-in build cost for a standard three-unit terrace in greater Christchurch has settled at around $4,200 to $4,800 per square metre, depending on cladding and fitout. That is roughly flat year-on-year after the steady increases of 2023 and 2024.

Consent timelines

Resource consents on controlled-activity multi-unit work are running at 35 to 60 working days at Christchurch City Council. Building consents add another 20 to 25 working days. Plan accordingly: a clean three-unit site, lodged today, is unlikely to be on site before Q1 2027.

Yields

Three-bedroom unit gross yields in middle-belt suburbs (Halswell, Riccarton, Linwood) sit at 4.6 to 5.2 percent on completed value. Net yields, after rates and body corp, land at roughly 3.8 to 4.4 percent. Outer suburbs add 0.3 to 0.5 percent.

What we are watching

The Long-Term Plan and the Plan Change 14 medium-density rules are settling. We are not expecting big movements either way in the next twelve months. Material costs are the main variable: long-run roof and aluminium joinery prices have been notably steady, timber has crept up 3 to 4 percent.
