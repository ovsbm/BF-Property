For land owners

Land with development potential.
We assess it. You decide.

If you own land in Canterbury that could carry more than one dwelling, BF Property will write you a free assessment. The result is yours regardless of what you do next.

Three paths

After the assessment you have three options. All three are normal outcomes.

Option one

Sell to BF Property

If the site fits a current pipeline we make an offer at market value. Cash settlement. You retain the assessment.

Option two

Develop together

You keep ownership of the land. BF builds at a fixed price, you receive the completed units, the partnership ends at handover. No equity stake taken.

Option three

Walk away with the note

The written assessment is yours. Use it with another builder, a buyer, a developer. We charge nothing for the first feasibility.
