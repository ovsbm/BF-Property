Questions

How is BF Property different from a volume builder?

Volume builders sell off-plan homes to first-home buyers and operate at scale. BF Property works with one client at a time, on capital they supply, to deliver an income-producing or owner-occupied asset. Different model, different audience.

Do you do single homes, or only multi-unit work?

Both. A custom single home is one of our service tracks. Most of the work, though, is multi-unit because that is what the audience we serve typically wants from their land.

What is the smallest project you take on?

The smallest project we take on is roughly $1m in build cost. Below that the senior-team-on-every-project model does not pencil for either side.

Do you source land?

Yes. If you have capital and you want a development outcome but no specific site, we will source land and run a feasibility. The land assessment service is a separate page.

How long does a typical three-unit development take?

From signed contract to handover, fourteen months is typical for a Canterbury three-unit on a clean site. Heritage, retaining walls, or hill builds extend that.

Are your contracts fixed price?

Yes. After detailed design and consent we issue a fixed-price head contract. The feasibility phase before that is indicative.

Who actually runs my project on a day-to-day basis?

A senior site lead from BF Property. The same person from foundation pour to handover. You also have a director contact for any decision above contingency.

Do you take an equity stake in my land or my development?

No. We are a contracted builder-developer. Your land stays yours, the contract is a build contract, and we are paid against milestones.

Are you Master Builders and Licensed Building Practitioners?

Yes to both, across the directors and the site team. Site Safe accredited. Homes delivered under the Halo ten-year guarantee.

What does the feasibility note actually contain?

One page. Indicative build cost, programme, yield or sale appraisal where relevant, and an honest list of red flags we have spotted. No charge for the first one.

Can I see a project under construction?

Yes, by appointment and with the current client\u2019s permission. We do not run a showhome.
