About BF Property

A senior Canterbury team, twenty-two years in the ground.

BF Property is the trading brand of a build-and-develop team that has been working under the buildFIRST name since 2004. The brand changed. The team didn't.

The story

The work was always direct-to-client. The name now says so.

buildFIRST started in 2004 as a small Canterbury build company. The work, from the beginning, was unusual. Not spec homes for the open market. Not house-and-land packages. The clients were people with capital who wanted a finished asset they didn't have to project-manage themselves.

Over twenty-two years the team grew, the projects grew, and the model held. Single homes became townhouse pairs. Pairs became three- and four-unit terraces. Then medium-density. Always for an identified client. Always handed over complete.

In 2025 the trading brand changed to BF Property to make that model explicit. Same director. Same senior team. Same workshop in Christchurch.

The model

A short description, written plainly.

BF Property is a direct-to-client contract developer. We take on one to twelve units at a time, across greater Christchurch, on capital you supply. Our senior team holds the head contract from foundation to handover. We coordinate the architect rather than author the design, and we source land if you don't have it.

It usually fits people who have built up a position in Canterbury property and are deciding what to do next with a site, an inheritance, or a development paddock that has been on their mind for a while.

It usually doesn't fit a first-home buyer, a buyer looking for the cheapest possible square-metre rate, or a project outside Canterbury. We say so on a first call rather than later.
