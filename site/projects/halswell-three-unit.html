The brief

Three units for long-term tenancy on a corner lot.

The owners had held the corner section since 2008. After the rebuild years the original home was tired and the site was clearly under-used. They wanted income, not a sale.

The brief: three three-bedroom townhouses, sized for working professionals, finished to the standard that lets a property manager set rents at the top of the suburb range without arguing.

The work

What BF Property did.

We bought the existing dwelling demolition into the consent so the programme was uninterrupted. Resource consent was a controlled activity given the corner location and proximity to a Significant Tree.

Build was eleven months from foundation pour to handover of unit three. The owners signed off at three formal gates: design lock, pre-line, and handover. Outside those, they were uninvolved.

The outcome

Outcome.

All three units were tenanted within four weeks of handover at the rents we had modelled. Gross yield landed at 5.1 percent, slightly above the feasibility estimate. The owners retain all three titles.

Body corp set up at settlement. AGM 1 ran two months after handover.
