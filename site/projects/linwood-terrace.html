The brief

Four two-bedroom units on a Linwood lot, designed for a single-owner retention model.

Owner had inherited the site and wanted to use it as a long-term yield asset. The site supported four units under Medium Residential zoning. The brief was deliberately modest: two-bedroom units, hard-wearing finishes, designed to handle a 25-year tenancy cycle.

The work

Where BF Property is.

Resource consent granted in February. Building consent granted in May. Demolition of the existing weatherboard cottage scheduled for late July.

Build kicks off in August with a 14-month programme to handover of unit four.
