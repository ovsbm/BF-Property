What it is

We are not the architect. We are the team that builds what the architect drew.

Custom design is a slower track than multi-unit work. The brief is unusual, the detailing is bespoke, the site is often constrained. BF Property's role is to coordinate the architect, engineer, and trades, and then to build to a level of finish that does the drawings justice.

We work with an architect you choose, or we recommend one from our list. The relationship is collaborative, not adversarial.

Programmes are typically twelve to twenty-four months from brief to handover.

What is included

What we run, what stays with the architect.

  1. BF runs

    Site assessment

    Build cost from concept onwards, programme, consent risk.

  2. Architect runs

    Design

    Concept, developed and detailed design. We sit in the meetings.

  3. BF runs

    Consent

    Resource and building consent lodgement and tracking.

  4. BF runs

    Build

    Head contract, head subcontractor coordination, weekly programme tracking.

  5. Architect runs

    Site observation

    Architect attends key milestones for quality assurance.

  6. BF runs

    Handover

    Defects period, owner familiarisation, final architect sign-off.

When this fits

Best fits for custom design.

This fits if

  • You have engaged or are engaging an architect.
  • The home is for your own long-term occupation.
  • Budget supports detailed finishes and bespoke joinery.
  • You value coordination above all-in cost.

This is not right if

  • You want a standard plan from a builder catalogue.
  • You want the cheapest possible build cost. Custom adds 25 to 40% over standard.
  • You are unsure whether you want an architect at all.
